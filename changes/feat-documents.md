### Added

- **Linear documents, synced beside the issues.** The same `sync` copies them into the local store. `folders add` chooses
  the projects (`linear:all` for every project and the documents outside them), and `documents list|show|export`,
  `search`, `catalog` and the notes, tags and memories groups read them offline. `search all` finds them in the
  notes lane as kind `document`. A document Linear trashed or archived is removed; one that is only missing from a
  listing keeps its text.
